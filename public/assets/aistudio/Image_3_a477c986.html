Engineering Protocol

10-Stage Execution Timeline

Our documented methodology for swift clearance, structural endurance, and zero DISCOM net-meter delays.

01

Initial Discovery & Load Analysis

Analysis of past 12-month consumer electricity bills, sanction load, and tariff category.

02

Drone / 3D Roof Shadow Mapping

Obstacle height checks (parapets, trees, overhead tanks) to eliminate azimuth shadow losses.

03

CAD Structural & Electrical Engineering

Single Line Diagram (SLD), string layout, and cyclone-rated (200 km/h) mounting structure design.

04

DISCOM Net-Metering Feasibility

Online application filing on National Portal & TPCODL / TPNODL / TPWODL / TPSODL portals.

05

Tier-1 Hardware Dispatch & Delivery

ALMM-approved modules and inverters shipped with batch test certificates to site.

06

Hot-Dip Galvanized Mounting

80+ micron HDG structure anchoring with chemical fastener grouting to prevent roof leakage.

07

Inverter & AC/DC DB Protection Cabling

UV-resistant solar DC cables, Type-II surge arrestors (SPD), and miniature circuit breakers (MCBs).

08

Dual Chemical Earthing & Megger Testing

Dedicated earthing pits for AC, DC, and lightning arrestor (LA) ensuring resistance < 2 Ohms.

09

DISCOM Joint Inspection & Net Meter Sync

DISCOM SDO inspection, bi-directional net meter installation, and official grid sync.

10

Handover & IoT Mobile App Telemetry Setup

Subsidy documentation final submission, customer portal access, and lifetime warranty handover.
